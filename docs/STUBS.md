## Not stubs, but worth stating plainly

These are not fake — they simply do not exist yet, and no code pretends
otherwise: market data, any downloader, any database, the security master, both
backtesters, any strategy, any broker connection. See `STATE.md`.
| S-026 | 8 AMFI schemes have no NAV history at all | `data/landing/mf/history/` | Not a fetch failure — mfapi.in returns `{"data":[]}` with `scheme_code:0` for them. All 8 are recently launched (Invesco India Nifty Bank ETF, JioBlackRock Nifty Midcap 150 Index Fund and similar): AMFI already publishes their NAV, the community mirror has not caught up. This is precisely the case ADR-038 anticipated — **where AMFI and mfapi.in disagree, AMFI wins** — and the daily snapshot accumulates their history from today forward | resolves itself as the mirror catches up, or as daily snapshots accumulate |
| S-027 | Four windows of minute bars are absent from the crypto archive | `data/landing/crypto/` | **Not data loss — Binance outages.** The missing minutes are *identical timestamps across every symbol* and form single contiguous blocks: 2021-08-13 02:00-06:29 UTC (270 min), 2021-09-29 07:00-08:59 (120 min), 2023-03-24 12:40-13:59 (80 min). A per-symbol gap would look nothing like this, and `make check-crypto` distinguishes the two. The fourth window is **SOLUSDT 2023-02-14 16:39 UTC, a single minute**, affecting no other symbol — correctly reported as symbol-specific rather than an outage. It is benign: the bars either side both close at 21.7400, so no price movement was lost. Across the complete 1,200 symbol-months: zero OHLC, ordering or positivity violations, and zero bad prints — all 74 large moves are corroborated by volume | not fixable; the exchange was down |
| S-028 | No cost model covers crypto or mutual funds | `src/eulerlab/api/server.py` (`COSTED_CLASSES`) | `costs/schedule.py` has segments for equity delivery, equity intraday, futures and options — all Indian exchange-traded. A Binance spot trade pays a taker fee and a fund redemption may pay an exit load; neither pays STT, stamp duty or NSE charges. Running them through `EQUITY_DELIVERY` would produce a confidently wrong number, so those runs execute in `replication` mode — **gross of costs** — and the result carries `cost_mode` so it can never be compared to a net one unnoticed. This is a real limitation, not a rounding decision: a momentum rule on funds turned over its capital 27 times, where omitted costs are the difference between a result and a fiction | needs published Binance fee tiers and per-scheme exit loads, sourced into `docs/RESEARCH/` before anything is implemented |
| S-029 | About 2.8% of FXCM minute bars are crossed | `data/landing/fx/` | Ask below bid at the open or close, by up to a pip. Measured on EURUSD: 1,939 of 68,327 sampled bars, spread evenly across trading hours rather than clustered at rollover, so it is not a session artefact. This is how aggregated broker OHLC behaves — the recorded bid open and ask open can come from different ticks or different liquidity providers within the same minute. **The landed data is left exactly as served**, because raw data is immutable and a corrected file could never be checked against the source. The daily panel takes the *median* of a day's spreads, which every pair keeps positive (0.20 to 1.05 pips), and floors it at zero besides: a negative cost would pay a strategy to trade, which is a worse error than any overestimate | not fixable at source; the floor is a guard and the median is the real defence |
| S-030 | US prices are raw — no split or dividend adjustment is applied anywhere | `src/eulerlab/corp_actions/alpaca_feed.py` | The Alpaca corporate-actions feed was built and wired to nothing: no module imports it, so the US panel serves exchange prices as recorded. Unlike the NSE side, which has a factor store and `make check-adjust`, there is no adjusted US series at all. **This does not affect any current US result**, and the reason is structural rather than lucky: every US strategy on the platform runs through `intraday/bracket.py`, which forces a close at `session_end_index`, so no position ever spans the overnight boundary a split or dividend is applied across. That invariant is now asserted rather than assumed — `tests/test_bracket_is_flat_overnight.py`, whose first version passed with the forced close deleted and had to be rebuilt around a fixture that actually reaches it. The gap becomes real the moment a US strategy holds overnight, and it will be silent when it does: unadjusted prices show a split as a genuine-looking gap | needed before the first multi-day US strategy, not before |
