# ASSUMPTIONS — everything this project rests on but has not verified

Every entry has a recheck date. Anything here that turns out to be wrong
invalidates whatever depends on it, so the "Depends on it" column matters as
much as the claim.

Regulatory and contract-spec facts change often. Treat a passed recheck date as
a red flag, not a formality.

**How to use this file:** `grep -rn "ASSUMPTION:" src/` finds every place in the
code that rests on one of these.

| A-005 | STT on option exercise is charged on notional, not premium | **Resolved 2026-08-19 — TRUE until 2019-08-31, FALSE since.** The playbook recorded a fact that had expired. Effective 2019-09-01 STT on exercise is charged on **intrinsic value** (settlement minus strike), not the notional settlement value. The difference is a factor of ~289 on a near-the-money expiry: Rs 1,084 vs Rs 3.75 on Zerodha's published example. **Both rules are implemented** and selected by trade date (ADR-020), because the notional rule governs the first four and a half years of our price history. The cash-settlement and physical-delivery halves of A-005 remain unverified and are still open. |
| A-002 | Index lot sizes are periodically reset and must be time-versioned | **Resolved 2026-08-19 — CONFIRMED from primary data.** Built a contract registry from 140 monthly F&O bhavcopy snapshots (2.29M contracts, 5.7M contract-days) and read NIFTY's lot size through time: **50** (2024-03), **25** (2024-11), **75** (2025-03), **65** (2026-03). The playbook's claimed 25→75→65 is right as far as it goes and omits the earlier 50. Lot size is now derived from our own data rather than assumed, and `ContractRegistry.lot_size_at()` answers it per date. |

Read directly at `nseindia.com/static/nse-terms-of-use`. robots.txt is permissive but does not override written terms. **Superseded by ADR-006:** the operator was shown this and decided to proceed anyway, knowingly. | A-018 | The kill-switch limits in `config/killswitch.yaml` are appropriate | **UNVERIFIED, and knowingly so.** ₹10,000 daily loss, 200 orders/day, 5 consecutive rejections, ₹500,000 per position, 3 unresolved orders. Every one is a round number chosen to be conservative against the 1–2% of capital Sprint 31 puts at risk; none is derived from a measured distribution. The loss limit is the one that matters — set too tight it halts on an ordinary day, set too loose it does not halt at all. **Recheck: after four weeks of paper trading**, from the observed daily P&L distribution rather than from intuition. `# ASSUMPTION:` noted in the config file itself. |
| A-019 | The hosted providers in `config/llm.yaml` offer usable free tiers | **UNVERIFIED, and deliberately not claimed.** OpenRouter's `:free` models, Groq's free tier and similar are advertised by their vendors; none has been tested from this machine and none is quoted as a specific quota anywhere in the code. What *is* relied on is the request shape — `POST {base_url}/chat/completions` with `{model, messages}` — which is the common OpenAI-compatible interface all of them publish. The only provider with genuinely no quota is `ollama`, because it runs locally. **Recheck: whenever a hosted provider is actually used.** Nothing in the system depends on any of them; the default needs no key at all. |
| A-020 | The effective number of independent trials can be estimated by clustering correlated strategy return series (ONC / hierarchical / spectral methods on the correlation matrix) | **UNVERIFIED — no primary source obtained.** Two independent search summaries attribute this to Lopez de Prado (2018), but the Wikipedia article 404s and the Bailey & Lopez de Prado deflated-Sharpe PDF extracted badly enough to be unusable (it returned formulas matching neither the literature nor our own implementation; see research note 015). What *is* sourced is the direction: Harvey, Liu & Zhu (2016) §4 treat correlation among test statistics as a first-class problem and model it explicitly. Note that `features/independence.py` already performs this computation for features without needing the reference. | Low | **Before ADR-035 is implemented** | ADR-035's effective-N correction. The whole fleet-scale accounting rests on it |
| A-021 | `variance_of_sharpes = 1.0` — the dispersion of Sharpe ratios across our trials is 1.0 | **UNVERIFIED, and never once overridden.** It is a default argument in `expected_max_sharpe()` that appears nowhere in `docs/`. It **scales the entire noise bar**: at sigma_SR 0.5 the bar at 19 trials is 0.94, at 1.0 it is 1.88, at 1.5 it is 2.82. The project's best-ever result is **1.27**, which sits inside that range — so this constant is potentially the difference between "the gate has killed everything it has seen" and "something survived", a sentence that appears as settled fact in `README.md`, `STATE.md` and `docs/HISTORY.md`. | Low | **Attempted 2026-08-20 and it CANNOT yet be measured; now instrumented so it will be.** Only 3 of 19 ledger entries carry a Sharpe (`validation` kind); the 12 `ic` and 4 `gate` entries record none. All 3 are the same subject, H-0001 at successive stages (1.0088, 1.0088, 1.2667) — one hypothesis measured three times, not a cross-section. Their naive sd of 0.149 would imply a bar of 0.28, which is measuring the wrong thing rather than measuring it badly. `ledger.sharpe_dispersion()` now measures it and refuses while the evidence is this thin; `validation/run.py` records the canonical `sharpe` key going forward. Dispersion is taken across **distinct subjects**, not entries — repeats of one hypothesis would report re-measurement noise as the spread between ideas and shrink sigma, which lowers the bar. Needs 5 distinct subjects. Applies going forward only; the 19 existing entries cannot be back-filled. Recheck once 5 distinct hypotheses have been validated | Every DSR, every MinBTL, every gate verdict the project has ever issued. ADR-035 names this a precondition |

## Standing caveat on the reference documents

`docs/quant_platform_playbook.md` states in its own header that all regulatory
and tax details are as of August 2026 and must be re-verified against primary
sources before being relied on. Every A-00x entry above that cites it inherits
that caveat. **No number from these documents should reach a cost model, a
margin calculation, or a contract spec without a primary source first.**

| A-025 | Delta Exchange fees are the published retail rates, with no tier or token discount | `src/eulerlab/costs/delta_exchange.py` | Read 2026-08-22 from delta.exchange/fees and the support article, which agree on futures: 0.02% maker, 0.05% taker, plus 18% GST on the fee. Volume tiers and the DETO token discount both exist and are **not** modelled, so every cost here is an upper bound for a trader who qualifies for either. Recheck quarterly — a published fee schedule is exactly the kind of fact that changes without announcement | 2026-11-22 |
| A-026 | Options fees are not modelled because two official pages disagree | `src/eulerlab/costs/delta_exchange.py` | The fees page says 0.03% with a cap written as "10% 3.5% of Option Premium", evidently stale; the support article says 0.010% of notional capped at 3.5% of premium. Modelling a number the venue contradicts itself on would be worse than declining to | when the pages agree |
| A-027 | Indian VDA tax is not a transaction cost and is not modelled | `src/eulerlab/costs/delta_exchange.py` | 30% on gains and 1% TDS under section 194S apply to realised gains at assessment, not per trade. Putting them inside a backtest would confuse a levy on outcome with a cost of execution. Whether 194S reaches derivatives at all is a regulatory question this module does not answer and must not appear to | needs a sourced reading of 194S before any attempt |
| A-028 | Perpetual funding is not modelled | `src/eulerlab/costs/delta_exchange.py` | Paid every eight hours by whichever side is long at the mark. An intraday strategy flat by the close may pay none; one straddling a stamp pays all of it. The platform holds price only, no funding rate, so this cannot be modelled from what is landed | needs a funding-rate feed |
| A-029 | US equity execution costs are Alpaca's zero commission plus the two sell-side regulatory pass-throughs | `src/eulerlab/costs/us_equity.py` | Verified 2026-08-25. Section 31: **$0.00 per million through 2026-04-03, $20.60 from 2026-04-04**, per [FINRA Information Notice 03/17/26](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/information-notice-20260317) — the SEC's own advisory page returns 403 to automated fetches, so the regulator's republication of the order is the primary source used. FINRA TAF: $0.000166 per share sold, capped $8.30 per trade. Modelled as a **dated schedule, not a constant**: the first draft hardcoded $27.80, which is the fiscal *2025* rate and would have overcharged every 2026 backtest by a third and charged a January 2026 trade for a fee that was zero. Dates before 2025-10-01 raise `RateUnavailable` rather than being priced with an unverified rate. Recheck when fiscal 2027 appropriations pass — the rate resets 60 days after | at FY2027 appropriation |
| A-030 | Spread and slippage are not modelled on any venue, so all three cost models are lower bounds | `src/eulerlab/costs/us_equity.py`, `delta_exchange.py`, `schedule.py` | Fills come from minute bars, and a bar's high and low are not a book. Adding a modelled half-spread would put an invented number beside measured ones and make the total look more precise than its worst component. The omission is **consistent across NSE, Delta and US**, so the three remain comparable to each other — but every net figure this platform reports is better than a real fill would be, and the gap is largest exactly where it matters most: US friction is now ~0.002% of notional, which is far below a realistic QQQ spread | needs quote-level data to model honestly |
| A-031 | IEX minute volume is a usable proxy for US consolidated volume | `src/eulerlab/intraday/us_store.py` | **UNVERIFIED and probably false for volume specifically.** The US minute bars are the Alpaca IEX feed — one venue, carrying a low single-digit share of consolidated tape. Prices track closely; **volume does not**. Three swept signals key on volume — `volume_spike`, `volume_dryup`, `volume_climax` — and their thresholds are reading one venue's participation rather than the market's. All three lost money in the first US sweep, which is consistent with the signal being real and the input being wrong, and equally consistent with the signal not being real. This assumption cannot be resolved without the SIP feed | needs the SIP feed, which is paid |
| A-032 | QQQ cash bars are an acceptable stand-in for MNQ futures | `src/eulerlab/optimize_run.py` | Nine coded signals cite Mesfin (2026), tested on MNQ — the Micro E-Mini Nasdaq 100 future. This platform holds no futures data, so they are swept on QQQ, which tracks the same underlying index. **The index is the same; the instrument is not.** No roll, no overnight margin, no futures tick size, different session boundaries (MNQ trades nearly 23 hours, QQQ cash 6.5), and different cost structure. The substitution happens in `market_for` rather than inside the loader specifically so it is visible and named. Before this, those nine were swept on `RELIANCE`, which was worse and silent | when futures data is landed |
| A-033 | An adopted parameter is the better guess of those tried, not a validated result | `src/eulerlab/intraday/tuned.py` | **UNVERIFIED by the platform's own bar, and deliberately labelled as such.** Adoptions come from a recorded 180-trial sweep over a 58-session window. None clears t > 3.0 or DSR 0.95; `orb_in_play` rests on 12 fills, `gap_fill_fade` on 17, `orb_breakout` on 23. The value is stored with its best, median, fill count and tail share so it cannot be read without them, and ADR-044 sets the three conditions under which adoption happens at all. **Recheck: any adopted parameter that has not been re-swept on a longer window should be treated as provisional** | when a window beyond 58 sessions exists |
| A-034 | Re-sweeping the same grid re-spends trials, and that is correct | `src/eulerlab/optimize.py`, `features/trials.py` | Running `make optimize` twice on the same space records 180 trials each time, so the denominator grows even though no new ground was searched. That over-counts. The alternative — de-duplicating on the parameter signature — under-counts far worse: the same grid on a later window is a genuinely new test, and a searcher who re-runs until a result appears would pay nothing for it. The ledger errs toward the bar being too high, which is the only direction that does not flatter | standing |
| A-035 | US trades are billed at INDmoney's schedule, with Firstrade priced alongside | `src/eulerlab/costs/us_equity.py` | Verified 2026-08-26. INDmoney: **0.25% per leg, capped at $25 per transaction, plus 18% GST** — a flat $29.50 a leg above $10,000 of notional. Firstrade: **$0 commission online**, paying only the SEC Section 31 fee and FINRA TAF, both sell-side, about $10.47 on a $500,000 sell. `EULERLAB_US_BROKER` selects; every US result carries both. **The cap is the whole model**: uncapped, 0.25% a leg costs 0.5% a round trip and no signal in this library survives it — friction on `orb_breakout` reached 309% of gross edge. Capped, the same round trip is 0.012% and US is the cheapest venue here. This module claimed three different things in sequence before the cap was known; a fee model missing its cap is not conservative, it is wrong by a factor of fifty | when either broker republishes |
| A-036 | Currency conversion is a cost on capital, not on trades, and is not modelled | `src/eulerlab/costs/us_equity.py` | INDmoney quotes 0.5%-1.2% INR→USD, charged by the remitting bank. Paid when money crosses the border, not when a position opens: a strategy that funds once and trades a hundred times pays it twice, not two hundred times. Charging per fill would inflate an active strategy's costs by its own turnover. **Consequence: every US result is gross of roughly ₹5,000-12,000 each way on a ₹10 lakh account**, regardless of performance. TCS at 20% above ₹10L is also absent, being creditable against income tax — it blocks capital rather than consuming it, the same reasoning as A-027 | needs a capital-flow model, not a trade-cost one |
| A-037 | The NSE minute archive stores overlapping chunks, so the reader must deduplicate | `src/eulerlab/intraday/store.py` | Found 2026-08-26.
