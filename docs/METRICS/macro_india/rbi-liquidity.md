# Banking system liquidity (LAF balance)

*Family: Indian economy. Id: `rbi-liquidity`.*

**In one line:** Whether banks have surplus cash parked with RBI or are short and borrowing from it.

**Where you see it:** Not on the workbench.

**How we compute it:** RBI's daily liquidity adjustment facility (LAF) operations: net absorption means surplus, net injection means deficit.

**Data and freshness:** RBI daily money market operations release.

**Do we hold it:** No.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Surplus liquidity tends to keep short-term rates near the floor and supports bond prices; a deficit pushes them toward the ceiling.
- Large tax outflows and RBI's currency intervention move liquidity in predictable seasons.

**What it does not tell you:** The policy stance itself.

**Traps:**

- Rules and calendars change; check the official page before quoting a date or a rate (A-085).

## Example answers

**Q:** Liquidity turned deficit. Does it matter for equities?

**A:** It mostly shows up first in money market and bond yields; for equities it matters through banks' funding costs and, if prolonged, credit growth.
