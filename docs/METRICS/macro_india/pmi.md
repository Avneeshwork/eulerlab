# PMI (manufacturing, services, composite)

*Family: Indian economy. Id: `pmi`.*

**In one line:** A monthly survey of purchasing managers; above 50 means activity expanded on the month.

**Where you see it:** Economic calendar.

**How we compute it:** HSBC India PMI compiled by S&P Global; headline manufacturing PMI is a weighted average of five sub-indices (new orders 30%, output, employment, delivery times, stocks). A flash estimate comes about a week before the final.

**Data and freshness:** S&P Global release, early in the month at 10:30 IST.

**Do we hold it:** No, unless listed on the economic calendar.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** 50 is the line between expansion and contraction.

**How to read it:**

- It is about breadth of improvement, not size: 58 means more firms reported growth, not 8% growth.

**What it does not tell you:** Output levels.

**Traps:**

- Composite PMI is not comparable with the headline manufacturing PMI (S&P Global).

## Example answers

**Q:** Manufacturing PMI 56.2, down from 57.5.

**A:** Factories are still expanding, a little less widely than last month. Not a contraction signal.
