# CPI inflation (retail)

*Family: Indian economy. Id: `cpi-inflation`.*

**In one line:** Year-on-year change in consumer prices; the inflation measure RBI targets.

**Where you see it:** Economic calendar, when held.

**How we compute it:** Released by MoSPI on the 12th of each month at 16:00 IST (next working day if a holiday); new series base 2024 = 100 from February 2026.

**Data and freshness:** MoSPI press release.

**Do we hold it:** Only as a calendar event if ForexFactory lists it; the index itself is not stored.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** RBI's inflation target is 4% with a band of 2% to 6% (flexible inflation targeting; not fetched here, recheck on rbi.org.in).

**How to read it:**

- Above expectations: rate cuts less likely, yields up, rate-sensitive stocks down. Below: the reverse.
- Food inflation is volatile; core (excluding food and fuel) shows underlying pressure.

**What it does not tell you:** Producer costs (see WPI).

**Traps:**

- Rules and calendars change; check the official page before quoting a date or a rate (A-085).
- The 2026 base change means old and new series are not directly comparable.

## Example answers

**Q:** CPI came in at 5.8% vs 5.2% expected.

**A:** A higher-than-expected print makes an RBI cut less likely, which usually lifts bond yields and weighs on banks, NBFCs and real estate first. Check core inflation before drawing a trend.
