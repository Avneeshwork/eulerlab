# WPI inflation (wholesale)

*Family: Indian economy. Id: `wpi-inflation`.*

**In one line:** Year-on-year change in wholesale prices; a read on input costs for companies.

**Where you see it:** Economic calendar.

**How we compute it:** Published monthly by the Office of the Economic Adviser, Ministry of Commerce.

**Data and freshness:** Government release.

**Do we hold it:** No.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- WPI rising faster than CPI squeezes manufacturers' margins (they pay more than they can pass on).

**What it does not tell you:** RBI's target measure.

**Traps:**

- Rules and calendars change; check the official page before quoting a date or a rate (A-085).

**Related:**

- [cpi-inflation](../macro_india/cpi-inflation.md)

**Sources:**

- Office of the Economic Adviser (not fetched; general knowledge, check the release).

## Example answers

**Q:** WPI jumped but CPI is flat?

**A:** Input costs are rising faster than consumer prices, a margin risk for manufacturers next quarter.
