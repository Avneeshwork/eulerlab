# GDP growth

*Family: Indian economy. Id: `gdp-growth`.*

**In one line:** Quarterly growth of the economy's output.

**Where you see it:** Economic calendar.

**How we compute it:** MoSPI quarterly estimates, about two months after the quarter ends; new series base 2022-23 from February 2026.

**Data and freshness:** MoSPI release.

**Do we hold it:** No.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Matters for earnings over quarters; on the day, the surprise against forecasts and revisions to earlier quarters move markets.

**What it does not tell you:** Listed-company profits, which can diverge for years.

**Traps:**

- Rules and calendars change; check the official page before quoting a date or a rate (A-085).
- Release dates in the 2026-27 calendar were revised more than once.

**Related:**

- [iip](../macro_india/iip.md)
- [pmi](../macro_india/pmi.md)

**Sources:**

- MoSPI Advance Release Calendar 2026-27 (search summary, 2026-10-31).

## Example answers

**Q:** GDP grew 6.1% vs 6.8% expected.

**A:** A miss on growth; cyclical sectors (banks, capital goods, autos) are most exposed. Revisions to earlier quarters can matter as much as the headline.
