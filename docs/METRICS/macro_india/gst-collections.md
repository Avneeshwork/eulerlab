# GST collections

*Family: Indian economy. Id: `gst-collections`.*

**In one line:** Monthly goods and services tax revenue; a quick read on consumption and compliance.

**Where you see it:** Not on the workbench.

**How we compute it:** Finance Ministry release, usually on the 1st of the month.

**Data and freshness:** Government release.

**Do we hold it:** No.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Strong year-on-year growth suggests steady activity; rate changes by the GST Council distort comparisons.

**What it does not tell you:** Real growth (it includes inflation).

**Traps:**

- Rules and calendars change; check the official page before quoting a date or a rate (A-085).

- [gst-council](../policy/gst-council.md)

**Sources:**

- Not fetched.

## Example answers

**Q:** GST collections up 9% YoY.

**A:** Nominal growth, so part is inflation. Steady but not a strong signal on its own.
