# Index of Industrial Production (IIP)

*Family: Indian economy. Id: `iip`.*

**In one line:** Monthly growth in factory, mining and electricity output.

**Where you see it:** Economic calendar.

**How we compute it:** MoSPI, monthly (the 2026-27 calendar lists the 28th).

**Data and freshness:** MoSPI release.

**Do we hold it:** No.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- A monthly read on industry; noisy, so look at the three-month trend.

**What it does not tell you:** Services, most of the economy.

**Traps:**

- Rules and calendars change; check the official page before quoting a date or a rate (A-085).

## Example answers

**Q:** IIP fell 1%?

**A:** One weak month in a noisy series; check the three-month trend and the capital goods component.
