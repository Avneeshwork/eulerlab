# GST Council decisions

*Family: Government policy and regulators. Id: `gst-council`.*

**In one line:** Changes to indirect tax rates on goods and services.

**Where you see it:** Events.

**How we compute it:** The Council (Union and state finance ministers) meets several times a year.

**Data and freshness:** GST Council press releases.

**Do we hold it:** No.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- A rate cut on a product category helps the companies selling it (for example autos, consumer goods); a hike hurts.

**What it does not tell you:** Broad market direction.

**Traps:**

- Rules change; check the official circular or notification before quoting a rate or a date (A-085).

- [gst-collections](../macro_india/gst-collections.md)

**Sources:**

## Example answers

**Q:** GST on small cars cut.

**A:** Lower prices for buyers; supportive for auto makers in that segment, depending on how much they pass on.
