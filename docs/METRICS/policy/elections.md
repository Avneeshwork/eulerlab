# Elections

*Family: Government policy and regulators. Id: `elections`.*

**In one line:** National and major state elections and their results.

**Where you see it:** Events; high-volatility days.

**How we compute it:** Election Commission schedule.

**Data and freshness:** ECI.

**Do we hold it:** No.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Markets price policy continuity; surprise results cause the largest moves, often in PSU and infrastructure stocks.

**What it does not tell you:** Outcomes.

**Traps:**

- Exit polls move markets before results and can be wrong.

**Related:**

- [india-vix](../market/india-vix.md)

**Sources:**

- Not fetched.

## Example answers

**Q:** Why was VIX so high before results day?

**A:** Option buyers pay for protection against a surprise result, so implied volatility rises into the event and usually drops after it.
