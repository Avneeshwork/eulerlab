# Government schemes, PLI and disinvestment

*Family: Government policy and regulators. Id: `government-schemes-and-disinvestment`.*

**In one line:** Policies that direct money to sectors, and government stake sales in listed companies.

**Where you see it:** Events; deals data for offers for sale.

**How we compute it:** Production-linked incentive (PLI) schemes pay manufacturers for output growth; disinvestment sells government stakes, often through offers for sale (OFS).

**Data and freshness:** Ministry releases; NSE OFS notices.

**Do we hold it:** Bulk/block deals and IPOs via `make nse-reports`; OFS notices not separately.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- An OFS adds supply at a discount and usually weighs on the stock for a few days.

**What it does not tell you:** Long-term value.

**Traps:**

- Rules change; check the official circular or notification before quoting a rate or a date (A-085).

## Example answers

**Q:** Government announced an OFS in a PSU bank.

**A:** Extra shares come to market at a floor price below the last close; the stock usually trades near that floor until the sale is absorbed.
