# Foreign portfolio investor (FPI) rules

*Family: Government policy and regulators. Id: `fpi-rules`.*

**In one line:** Who may invest from abroad, in what category, and with what limits.

**Where you see it:** Explains the FII line in flows and positioning.

**How we compute it:** SEBI (Foreign Portfolio Investors) Regulations, 2019: two categories. Category I: governments, central banks, sovereign funds, multilateral bodies, pension and university funds, regulated entities from FATF countries. Category II: everything else (other funds, endowments, corporates, family offices, individuals).

**Data and freshness:** SEBI; NSDL FPI monitor.

**Do we hold it:** No.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- 'FII' and 'FPI' mean the same group in market reporting; FII is the older term.

**What it does not tell you:** Their trading intentions.

**Traps:**

- Rules change; check the official circular or notification before quoting a rate or a date (A-085).
