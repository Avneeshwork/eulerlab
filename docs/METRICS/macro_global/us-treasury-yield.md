# US 10-year Treasury yield

*Family: Global economy and markets. Id: `us-treasury-yield`.*

**In one line:** The world's benchmark risk-free rate.

**Where you see it:** Brief World section (US 10Y).

**How we compute it:** TradingView feed at brief time.

**Data and freshness:** Snapshot.

**Do we hold it:** Snapshot only.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Rising US yields make emerging-market assets less attractive by comparison; the gap between Indian and US yields matters for bond flows.

**What it does not tell you:** Indian rates directly.
