# Crude oil (Brent, WTI)

*Family: Global economy and markets. Id: `crude-oil`.*

**In one line:** The price of oil; India imports most of what it uses.

**Where you see it:** Brief World section; MCX data.

**How we compute it:** TradingView feed; MCX minute bars for Indian futures.

**Data and freshness:** Snapshot; MCX via Kite.

**Do we hold it:** Snapshot; MCX minute bars are held (research 082).

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Higher crude: costlier imports, weaker rupee, higher inflation risk; hurts oil marketers, paints, airlines, tyres; helps upstream producers (ONGC, Oil India).

**What it does not tell you:** Domestic fuel prices, which are partly administered.

**Traps:**

- India's crude basket is priced mainly off Middle East grades and Brent, not WTI (not fetched; check PPAC).

**Q:** Brent up 2.4% to $113.

**A:** A cost for India: weighs on the rupee, inflation expectations and oil-consuming sectors, and helps upstream producers.
