# US Federal Reserve (FOMC) rate decisions

*Family: Global economy and markets. Id: `us-fed`.*

**In one line:** The US policy rate; the single biggest driver of global money moving into or out of emerging markets.

**Where you see it:** Economic calendar (ForexFactory, high impact).

**How we compute it:** Set by the FOMC, which meets eight times a year; decisions at 14:00 US Eastern, which is late night or early morning IST.

**Data and freshness:** Federal Reserve statement, projections and press conference.

**Do we hold it:** As calendar events, when the ForexFactory feed is current.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Higher US rates and a stronger dollar tend to pull foreign money out of emerging markets including India; cuts tend to do the reverse.
- The dot plot and press conference often move markets more than the decision.

**What it does not tell you:** India's growth story, which can attract money regardless.

**Traps:**

- The reaction shows in Indian markets the next morning (GIFT Nifty first).

## Example answers

**Q:** The Fed held but sounded hawkish. Effect on India?

**A:** A firmer dollar and higher US yields usually mean more FII selling pressure and a weaker rupee in the following sessions.
