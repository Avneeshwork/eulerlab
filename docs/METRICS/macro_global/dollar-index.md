# Dollar index (DXY)

*Family: Global economy and markets. Id: `dollar-index`.*

**In one line:** The dollar against six major currencies.

**Where you see it:** Brief World section when fetched.

**How we compute it:** TradingView feed.

**Data and freshness:** Snapshot.

**Do we hold it:** Snapshot only.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- A rising DXY usually means a weaker rupee and FII outflows.

**What it does not tell you:** USD/INR directly; the rupee is not in the index.
