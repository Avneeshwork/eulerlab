# GIFT Nifty and overnight global markets

*Family: Global economy and markets. Id: `gift-nifty`.*

**In one line:** Nifty 50 futures traded in GIFT City before India opens; the first read on the morning.

**Where you see it:** Not held yet.

**How we compute it:** A dollar-denominated Nifty 50 futures contract on NSE International Exchange, which replaced SGX Nifty in July 2023 and trades for most of the day.

**Data and freshness:** NSE IX.

**Do we hold it:** No.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Its gap to Nifty's last close suggests the opening direction, along with Asian markets and US futures.

**What it does not tell you:** Where Nifty closes.

**Traps:**

- Sources disagree on its exact session hours; check NSE IX.
- Indian residents generally cannot trade it.

## Example answers

**Q:** GIFT Nifty is 150 points down. Will Nifty open lower?

**A:** Usually it opens close to where GIFT Nifty points, but the pre-open auction sets the actual opening price.
