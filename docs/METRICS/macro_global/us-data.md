# US inflation and jobs data (CPI, payrolls)

*Family: Global economy and markets. Id: `us-data`.*

**In one line:** The US numbers that move Fed expectations, and so global markets.

**Where you see it:** Economic calendar (high impact).

**How we compute it:** US CPI from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, monthly; non-farm payrolls, usually the first Friday of the month, 18:00 or 19:00 IST depending on US daylight saving.

**Data and freshness:** ForexFactory calendar.

**Do we hold it:** As events.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Hot inflation or strong jobs: fewer Fed cuts expected, dollar up, EM stocks under pressure. Weak: the reverse.

**What it does not tell you:** Indian fundamentals.

**Traps:**

- Released during or after Indian market hours; Nifty reacts the next day.

**Related:**

- [us-fed](../macro_global/us-fed.md)

**Sources:**

- ForexFactory calendar (research 004 and ingest).

## Example answers

**Q:** US CPI hotter than expected last night.

**A:** Expect the dollar firm and pressure on Asian markets at the open; GIFT Nifty shows how much.
