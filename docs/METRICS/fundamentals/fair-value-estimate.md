# Fair-value estimate (residual income)

*Family: Company fundamentals. Id: `fair-value-estimate`.*

**In one line:** An internal estimate of value from book value, earnings, peers and the cost of equity.

**Where you see it:** Company page.

**How we compute it:** Residual-income model with factor betas shrunk toward neutral and the India 10-year yield (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, Fair-value estimates; research 117-119).

**Data and freshness:** Fundamentals, prices, IIMA factors.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- A rough cross-check, not a target price.

**What it does not tell you:** A recommendation.

**Traps:**

- Sensitive to the cost of equity and growth assumptions.

**Related:**

- [company-pe](../fundamentals/company-pe.md)

**Sources:**

- Research 117, 118, 119.

## Example answers

**Q:** Fair value 20% above the price?

**A:** Under this model's assumptions the stock looks cheap; small changes to the cost of equity can erase that gap.
