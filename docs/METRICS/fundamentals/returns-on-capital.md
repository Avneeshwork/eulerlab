# ROCE and ROE

*Family: Company fundamentals. Id: `returns-on-capital`.*

**In one line:** Profit earned on the capital the business uses, and on shareholders' equity.

**Where you see it:** Company page ratios.

**How we compute it:** As printed by the source (screener.in).

**Data and freshness:** Annual statements.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** Sector-dependent.

**How to read it:**

- Steady high ROCE with low debt is the sign of a good business.

**What it does not tell you:** Price attractiveness.

**Traps:**

- ROE rises with debt; read it with debt to equity.

## Example answers

**Q:** ROE 25% but ROCE 12%?

**A:** Returns to shareholders are boosted by borrowing; the business itself earns a modest return.
