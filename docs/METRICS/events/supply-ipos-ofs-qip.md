# New supply: IPOs, OFS, QIPs and lock-in expiries

*Family: Market events and calendar. Id: `supply-ipos-ofs-qip`.*

**In one line:** Fresh shares coming to market, which absorb money that might otherwise buy existing stocks.

**Where you see it:** Deals & filings tab (IPOs).

**How we compute it:** IPOs, offers for sale (promoter or government selling), qualified institutional placements (companies selling new shares to institutions), and the end of pre-IPO investors' lock-in.

**Data and freshness:** NSE reports.

**Do we hold it:** IPOs via `make nse-reports` (ADR-096).

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Heavy supply weeks can weigh on the broad market, especially mid and small caps.
- A lock-in expiry often pressures a recently listed stock.

**What it does not tell you:** Quality of the issues.

## Example answers

**Q:** A big IPO opens this week.

**A:** Large subscriptions can draw money from the secondary market for a few days, especially from smaller stocks.
