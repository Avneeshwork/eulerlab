# Index inclusion effects (stocks)

*Family: Market events and calendar. Id: `index-inclusion`.*

**In one line:** The price effect on a stock of being added to or removed from a major index.

**Where you see it:** Stocks to watch.

**How we compute it:** n/a

**Data and freshness:** Index provider announcements.

**Do we hold it:** No.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Much of the effect comes between announcement and effective date; it can reverse after.

**What it does not tell you:** Fundamentals.

## Example answers

**Q:** A stock jumped on index inclusion news.

**A:** Expected passive buying is being priced in; the forced buying itself happens at the close on the effective date.
