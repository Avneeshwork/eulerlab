# FIIs / FPIs: foreign investors

*Family: Institutions and money flows. Id: `fii-fpi`.*

**In one line:** Foreign funds, pension funds and sovereign investors buying Indian securities.

**Where you see it:** Cash flows panel; F&O positioning; brief.

**How we compute it:** See the FPI rules page for categories; NSE publishes their daily provisional cash buying and selling; NSDL publishes final FPI flows.

**Data and freshness:** NSE daily; NSDL.

**Do we hold it:** NSE daily cash flows (since 2025-10-01) and participant OI.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** See fii-dii-cash-flows: steady net sellers over the past year (median −₹1,305 cr a day).

**How to read it:**

- Their flows respond to global factors (Fed, dollar, emerging-market allocation, MSCI weights) as much as to India.
- Large FII selling with DII buying is the pattern of the past year.

**What it does not tell you:** Their full exposure (index futures shorts hedge cash longs).

**Traps:**

- NSE provisional and NSDL final figures differ (T-4239).

## Example answers

**Q:** Why do FIIs keep selling when India is growing?

**A:** Their flows follow global allocation: a strong dollar, high US yields or other markets' gains (for example Taiwan and Korea on AI) can pull money out regardless of India's growth.
