# Big banks and financials in the index

*Family: Institutions and money flows. Id: `big-banks`.*

**In one line:** Banks and financial companies are among the heaviest sectors in Nifty 50, so their results and RBI's rules move the index.

**Where you see it:** Sectors view (Nifty Financial Services, Nifty Bank, PSU Bank).

**How we compute it:** Sector indices from NSE; weights from NSE's factsheet (not fetched today).

**Data and freshness:** Index history.

**Do we hold it:** Sector indices yes; bank balance-sheet data only in company pages.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- Watch: loan (credit) growth against deposit growth, net interest margin (NIM), gross NPA (bad loans), and provisions.
- Rate cuts squeeze NIMs at first for banks with floating-rate loans; hikes widen them, until deposit costs catch up (RBI's FSR noted this lag in 2022-23).
- Bank Nifty and Nifty diverging shows whether financials are leading or lagging.

**What it does not tell you:** Individual bank quality.

**Traps:**

- Exact sector weights change with each rebalance; take them from NSE's latest factsheet.

## Example answers

**Q:** Bank Nifty fell 0.4% while Nifty rose 0.5%.

**A:** Financials lagged; the index gain came from other sectors. With financials so heavy in Nifty, that gap matters for where the index goes next.
