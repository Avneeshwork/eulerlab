# RBI and SEBI: who regulates what

*Family: Institutions and money flows. Id: `rbi-and-sebi`.*

**In one line:** RBI runs monetary policy, banks and the rupee; SEBI regulates markets, brokers, funds and listed companies.

**Where you see it:** Background for every policy page.

**How we compute it:** n/a

**Data and freshness:** rbi.org.in; sebi.gov.in circulars.

**Do we hold it:** Regulatory changes are logged in `docs/EVENTS/` when the monitor finds them.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** n/a

**How to read it:**

- A rate or banking rule is RBI; a trading, margin, F&O or disclosure rule is SEBI (then implemented by NSE/BSE).

**What it does not tell you:** Tax (Finance Ministry/CBDT).

## Example answers

**Q:** Who decides lot sizes?

**A:** SEBI sets the framework (minimum contract value); NSE sets each contract's lot size within it and revises them periodically.
