# DIIs: mutual funds, insurers, pension funds, banks

*Family: Institutions and money flows. Id: `dii-mutual-funds-insurers`.*

**In one line:** Domestic institutions; mostly mutual funds investing monthly SIP money, plus LIC and other insurers.

**Where you see it:** Cash flows panel; F&O positioning.

**How we compute it:** NSE's DII line combines domestic institutions. AMFI publishes monthly mutual fund flows.

**Data and freshness:** NSE daily; AMFI monthly.

**Do we hold it:** NSE daily (since 2025-10-01). AMFI no.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** DII net median +₹3,159 cr a day over the past year.

**How to read it:**

- Steady SIP inflows let mutual funds buy on most days, cushioning FII selling.

**What it does not tell you:** Retail investors who trade directly (they are in 'Client' in F&O data).

**Traps:**

- DIIs are not one decision maker; LIC alone can dominate a day.

## Example answers

**Q:** Why does the market not fall more when FIIs sell?

**A:** Domestic institutions, fed by monthly SIP inflows, have been buying on most days, absorbing much of the selling.
