# SIP flows and mutual fund inflows (AMFI)

*Family: Institutions and money flows. Id: `sip-flows`.*

**In one line:** Monthly money retail investors put into mutual funds through systematic plans.

**Where you see it:** Not on the workbench.

**How we compute it:** AMFI monthly report: gross SIP contributions, net equity fund inflows, AUM, folio counts.

**Data and freshness:** amfiindia.com, around the 10th of the month.

**Do we hold it:** No.

**What is normal (our data, as of 2026-10-30):** Gross SIP contributions were about ₹32,490 cr in September 2026 per secondary reports of AMFI data.

**How to read it:**

- Steady SIPs are the base of DII buying.
- Gross SIP is not net inflow: redemptions and other categories differ.

**What it does not tell you:** Direct retail trading.

**Traps:**

- Compare gross with gross and net with net; AMFI says they are not comparable.

**Related:**

- [dii-mutual-funds-insurers](../institutions/dii-mutual-funds-insurers.md)

**Sources:**

## Example answers

**Q:** SIP flows hit a record. Bullish?

**A:** It means steady domestic buying power, which has cushioned FII selling. It does not decide direction on any given day.
