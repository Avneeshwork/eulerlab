# ADR-193 — Every screened rule is scored forward each evening, and the backtest haircut is measured

**Date:** 2026-11-14. **Status:** accepted; operator's "do this also" on 2026-11-14 (#156). Tested on made-up data only; nothing is scheduled yet.

- **Context.** The platform did not know how much its backtests shrink out of sample, so a screen Sharpe meant nothing measurable, and forward evidence came only from books the operator started. Research 192: a swing book whose edge is gone is caught within a year only 11-23% of the time. Issue #156.
- **Options.** (a) Track only gated survivors (#131). (b) Re-score every rule the experiment store can rebuild, pass or fail, on the forward period, compute only, and regress forward on backtest Sharpe. (c) Wait for paper books.
- **Choice.** (b). `research/forward_shadow.py` builds the population from each experiment row's own `spec` (new: `experiments.record(spec=...)`, written by the research loop and `fast_eval.evaluate` from today), its card, or research 176's rules; cards first, then a sample of 400 fixed by fingerprint hash. Each evening it scores them on `LiveData` from `forward_start` (2026-01-01), with 400 days of warm-up history and no entry before the start, into `data/derived/forward_shadow.jsonl`, apart from the experiment store, which the agents read. The report (`make forward-haircut`) gives the slope of forward on backtest Sharpe with a bootstrap 95% interval, the rank correlation, the median share kept, and a breakdown by source, family, holding period and score quintile. `expected_live(sharpe)` answers only once 200 rules have 60 forward sessions (A-153).
- **Why.** It measures the research process, not one rule: whether a higher screen score predicts a higher forward return at all, and by how much backtests shrink, by idea source. Building it found HISTORY #91: the swing book would have dropped every swing after 2024-11-30 (no paper book was affected yet).
- **Cost if wrong.** Rows recorded before today have no spec and are lost unless a card holds the rule. A 400-rule evening costs about 20 minutes on one core at today's speed (#167). The haircut is not yet in the gate report: `gates.py` is the operator's, so it is only offered (T-4624). Not scheduled; needs the Mac's evening job or the server (T-4625).
