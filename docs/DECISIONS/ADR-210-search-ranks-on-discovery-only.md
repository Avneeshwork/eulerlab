# ADR-210 — A search ranks rules on discovery data only

**Context.** Issue #152. The constitution splits history into discovery (to 2023-12-31) and selection (2024-2025), and the firewall reports "holds on selection" as if the selection period were untouched. But evolve's fitness and the autopilot's screen were the net Sharpe over the engine's whole window, to `backtest_end`, so every survivor was chosen on the period that was then used to check it, and genetic search looks again every generation.
**Options.** (a) Keep the full-window screen and subtract the selection days afterwards; (b) cut the data itself at `discovery_end` for every search, and ask the selection period one coarse question after ranking; (c) drop the selection check.
**Choice.** (b). `rule_engine.through(data, end)` drops every bar after `end` (so a December swing cannot exit on a 2024 price) without changing the loaded data. Evolve, the autopilot's screen, the research loop and `make combine` screen on the cut data. `experiments.record` refuses a `screen` or `sweep` row that reads past `discovery_end`. `firewall.look` runs a clean rule once on the full data, reads only its selection days and records the one bit as a `selection` row with no returns. Evolve cards say so in `red_flags`.
**Why.** Option (a) still lets the search's numbers, parents and prompts carry 2024; cutting the data makes that impossible at the data layer, as the forward seal already is.
**Cost if wrong.** Screens see two fewer years (2024-2025), so a rule needs more discovery trades to pass and a few real but recent edges fail the screen. The autopilot loads the slow engine a second time for clean rules only (about one a night).
