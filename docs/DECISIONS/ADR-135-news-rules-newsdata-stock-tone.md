# ADR-135: News rules correct the tone and grow from judgements; NewsData is a second score; each stock gets a news tone

- **Context:** On 2026-11-04 the operator asked how wrong sentiment gets corrected when no AI tokens are left, for a knowledge base that grows over time and switches rules on automatically, for NewsData.io (their key) if better, for news sentiment on each stock, and for `make install` to stop removing the news model.
- **Options:** (a) Rely on the model's judgement and accept raw tone without it. (b) Written rules only. (c) Written seed rules plus rules learned by counting logged judgements, with NewsData as a second score and agreement reported.
- **Choice:** (c). `forecast/news_rules.py`: 13 seeds in `config/news_rules.yaml` (crude, rates, rupee, foreign flows, conflict, ceasefire, tariffs, inflation, with sector exceptions); every judged story logged to `data/derived/news_judgements.jsonl`; candidate rules (subject group × movement group, from stories no seed covers) rebuilt each run into `data/derived/news_rules_learned.json`, active at 3 agreeing and fewer than 2 against, off at 2 against; seeds are counted, never switched off, and listed when contested. With no model request, the rules read the stories. `ingest/market_news.py` adds NewsData `/latest` (key `News_Data_io`) for each search, keeping its sentiment as `newsdata_tone`; the brief reports how often the tone model, NewsData and the rules agree with the judged direction. `forecast/stock_tone.py` scores per-instrument headlines that name their stock, once each, corrects them with the rules and keeps 7-day tone per stock: shown in the brief (paper-book holdings, the most one-sided others) and on the company page. `make install` now includes the news model; `install-lite`, `install-news` and `remove-news` are separate.
- **Why:** (a) leaves wrong scores standing whenever the budget runs out. (b) cannot learn. (c) corrects with no model, learns only from counted evidence, and shows its own record.
- **Cost if wrong:** A rule learned from the model's judgements inherits the model's mistakes; three agreeing cases is a low bar, chosen by the operator ("keep automatic"), and every learned rule and change is listed in the brief. NewsData uses 10 of 200 free credits a refresh. Stock tone adds a read of two months of headline archives per brief.
