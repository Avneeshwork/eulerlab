# ADR-156 · Weekend sessions are collected; short sessions are held through, not traded

- **Context:** NSE held a Muhurat session on Sunday 2026-11-08 (14:20-15:30 IST). Every NSE downloader walked weekdays only, so no weekend session since 2015 was held, and the weekday Muhurat sessions that were held counted as full days in every backtest and paper book (research 183).
- **Options:** a hand-kept list of special sessions; probe weekends for the bhavcopy; probe weekends in every feed. For short days: treat as normal; drop from the panel; keep in the panel but never trade on them.
- **Choice:** the nightly bhavcopy step probes Saturdays and Sundays (`--include-weekends`, four extra requests a night); every other NSE feed (index closes, delivery, participant OI, reports, Kite minute bars) follows the weekend days whose cash bhavcopy landed (`nse_eod.session_days`, `weekend_sessions`). A short session is any day whose turnover is under 35% of the median of the 20 sessions before it (`master/sessions.py`). The daily engines (`vector.run`, `event_driven.run`, so paper books too) and the rule engine make no decision and no fill on a short session; positions are held through and marked at the next normal day. The bar stays in the panel and in signal history. `nse_calendar.json` lists `weekend_sessions` and `short_sessions`.
- **Why:** evidence, not a list, is how the calendar already works (`master/calendar.py`), and the next Muhurat needs no code change. Keeping the bar keeps the history true; refusing to trade it avoids deciding on a one-hour close and filling at a thin price.
- **Cost if wrong:** a genuinely tradable thin day is skipped (a hold delayed by a day), or a late-evening Muhurat bhavcopy lands after the 19:30 run and is picked up the next night.
