# ADR-153 — Live-price paper for intraday books, and a readiness checklist before real money

**Context.** Operator, 2026-11-08: a real-money intraday strategy by the end of November. The intraday paper books (H-0161, H-0175, H-0168) fill at the official open and close from the evening bhavcopy, the backtest's own assumption, so no number says what a real order would get; no live order has ever been sent (S-015); and the mandate asks 60 paper sessions before live (ADR-117).

**Options.** Wait for 60 paper sessions; send small live orders at once; or measure fills on live quotes without sending, and put every condition for real money on one checklist.

**Choice.** `deploy/live_shadow.py`: each evening a watchlist for the next session from signals known that night (a lag-k signal tomorrow is lag k-1 tonight); at 09:16 Kite quotes for the candidates, today's gap from the official open, the book's picks ranked as the rule ranks them, and the best bid and ask; at 15:10 quotes again; each evening the official close, and per trade the backtest's return, the return crossing the spread both times, and the gap between them (`data/live_shadow/summary.json`). It sends nothing. It shadows engine cards whose only same-morning signal is `gap` (H-0175) and rules restated in `config/live_shadow.yaml` (H-0161); H-0168 needs that morning's results and is listed as not supported. Launchd jobs at 09:16, 15:10 and 22:30. `deploy/readiness.py`: per strategy, an active paper book; at least the pilot's sessions of live-price paper with the edge still positive after real fills and 12.24 bps of costs; the Kite order path proved by one supervised order; a kill switch drill in 30 days; no open guardian hold; the operator's go naming the strategy; then either the mandate's `forward_test.pilot` block (absent until the operator adds it) or the full `forward_test`. It only reads.

**Why.** The edge that matters is the one left after real fills; it can be measured from the first session without risking money, while the mandate's longer test runs. One checklist makes "ready" a list of evidence, not a feeling.

**Cost if wrong.** Crossing the spread at the top of the book assumes it holds our size (A-123), so measured slippage is a floor. The H-0161 restatement uses the evening's liquid set, not the trade day's (A-124). The shadow reads 200 days of history, while paper books read nothing before the holdout end, so their picks can differ for the books' first 20 sessions. Every morning needs a fresh Kite login before 09:15, or the day is recorded as missed.
