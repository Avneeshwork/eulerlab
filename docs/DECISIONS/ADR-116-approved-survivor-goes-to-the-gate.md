# ADR-116: Approving an idea-lab survivor registers, validates and gates it

- **Context:** On 2026-11-01 the operator approved all 15 lab survivors and saw nothing happen. Approval only recorded a decision; each card still needed copying into `hypotheses/`, `make validate` and `make gate` by hand, so no lab idea ever reached the gate. Asked what should follow an approval, the operator chose "Gate automatically on approve" (recommended was a separate Send-to-gate tap).
- **Options:** (a) Keep it manual. (b) A Send-to-gate button after approval. (c) Approval itself registers the card and runs validation and the gate.
- **Choice:** (c), the operator's choice. `api/agent_routes.agents_decide` calls `research/promote`: an approved lab finding with survivors whose every configuration has a liquidity floor (ADR-112) and is not yet registered is written to `hypotheses/H-NNNN.yaml` (fresh id, configurations unchanged, status draft), then `python -m eulerlab.validation.run` and `python -m eulerlab.gate_run` run in their own processes, one at a time. The verdict goes to `data/agents/promote/<id>.json`, `GET /api/agents/promotions` and the Inbox's Gate runs panel. Validation now builds formula features and the liquidity floor through `codegen.build_one`, the lab's own builder.
- **Why:** The gate, not the lab, decides what is real; a survivor that never reaches it is wasted search. `gates.py` and the mandate are unchanged, validation logs itself as a trial, the gate writes its ledger entry, and `--read-holdout` is never passed.
- **Cost if wrong:** Every approval spends a validation trial and adds to the family count the bar is priced on; approving many survivors at once raises the bar for all of them. Cards tested before the floor are refused, so the 15 approved on 2026-11-01 are not gated; their ideas are retested with the floor instead. A validation reads the whole panel, so one run at a time can take many minutes each.
