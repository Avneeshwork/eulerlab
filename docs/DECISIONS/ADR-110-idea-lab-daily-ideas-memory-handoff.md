# ADR-110: The idea lab writes its own ideas daily, remembers every test, and trades feedback with research

- **Context:** On 2026-11-01 the operator asked for the idea lab to "bring lot of trading ideas daily", get "intelligent day by day" and "coordinate properly with research agent". Its first run tested nothing (HISTORY #47): it read only the latest research run, had no ideas of its own, kept no memory and told research nothing. The operator approved the plan in the thread the same day.
- **Options:** (a) Fix the draft reader only; ideas stay at about one a week. (b) A free formula search (mining). (c) A committed list of signal families, each with a reason written before testing, expanded into formula cards, steered by a memory of results, plus a few model-proposed formulas, with research and lab reading each other's outputs.
- **Choice:** (c). `config/idea_families.yaml` (13 families, 62 formulas); `research/idea_generator.py` plans a day's ideas: flips of signals that predicted the wrong way, combinations of two passers from different families, window variants of passers and near-passers (a third of the slots while fresh templates last, all of them after), then fresh templates by Thompson sampling on each family's Beta posterior; dates seed every draw. `research/idea_memory.py` keeps `data/agents/memory/lab_ideas.jsonl`, computes lessons that cite the experiences they rest on, and the feedback research reads: families working, dead and untried, features drafts lacked, paper topics in order. Research takes one lab topic a day when no brief names one and gets the lessons in its prompt; it no longer marks a buildable draft as waiting for data, and calls a draft a repeat when the lab already tested every feature. `make agents-ideas` runs research then the lab every day at 17:00 IST (`com.eulerlab.agentsideas`); the lab stops starting cards after 90 minutes.
- **Why:** Families keep a reason in front of every formula (Novy-Marx and Velikov warn that mining then explaining "industrialises HARKing", research 131); the bandit and lessons are the self-improvement research 131 found working (R&D-Agent(Q), ExpeL), without letting a model run code or invent a result. Every card is still a logged trial, read before the holdout, one configuration fixed before testing.
- **Cost if wrong:** About 30 more trials a day. The gate's bar counts families (ADR-108), so variants of one family cost little, but Romano-Wolf and the family count only see what has stored daily returns, and the lab's curves are in its run folders, not in workspaces (T-4310). Survivors of many tries are more likely lucky; the gate, holdout and forward paper still stand behind them. Speed on the full panel is unmeasured: about 3 seconds a card on a 54,000-row test panel, so the 90-minute limit may cut the first days short.
