# ADR-194 — The rule engine can price fills with a half spread and square-root impact on top of the reference print

**Date:** 2026-11-14. **Status:** accepted; operator's "do this also" on 2026-11-14 (#119). Tested on made-up data only; research 176 not yet re-priced on real data.

- **Context.** The engine fills at the official open and close with 2 bps a side (A-030, S-025). The official open is the pre-open auction, and until 3 Aug 2026 the official close was a 30-minute VWAP nobody could trade at. Research 184 found H-0202's edge lived only in that auction print, so every engine figure is an upper bound. Issue #119.
- **Options.** (a) Keep a flat 2 bps. (b) One pricer the books call: reference (official print, or minute VWAP 09:15-09:20 and 15:10-15:20 when minute bars are asked for) plus a half spread (measured from the depth recorder, else by traded-value bucket) plus k x daily vol x sqrt(order / 20-session mean traded value). (c) Full order-book replay, which needs history no free source sells (research 130).
- **Choice.** (b). `intraday/fill_price.PriceModel`, passed as `Engine(data, prices=...)` (and `FastEngine`); `daily_more_screen.day_book` and `swing_book` take it as `pricer`. The price moves against the trade by max(0, half spread + impact - 2 bps), so the 2 bps already charged is never charged twice. A modelled screen carries `price_model`; a default one is unchanged byte for byte, so research 176's parity holds. `make reprice` screens research 176's rules both ways and writes `data/derived/reprice.md`. Buckets (A-150) and k = 1 (A-151) are assumptions until the live-price shadow and paper fills calibrate them.
- **Why.** A rule whose edge is smaller than its real spread and impact should die in the screen, not in paper trading. Liquidity and volatility come from sessions before the fill only.
- **Cost if wrong.** The model is off by default: the research loop, evolve and the autopilot still screen on official prints until the operator turns it on (T-4627), so their figures stay upper bounds. The F&O closing auction from 3 Aug 2026 is not told apart from the old VWAP close. Minute VWAPs need the minute bars, which cover fewer years than the daily panel; without them the reference is the official print. Depth covers the Nifty 50 only, from the day the recorder first ran.
