# ADR-083: Published rules for the Trend and Participation cards

- **Context:** ADR-081's Trend (Nifty vs 200-day plus 3-month change) and Participation (60/40% above the 50-day) thresholds had no source. The operator asked what conditions published work uses for "Downtrend" and "Narrow" (research 122).
- **Options:** (a) keep ours; (b) the moving-average trio (price < 200-day, 50 < 200, 200 falling); (c) Faber's 10-month rule, as published; (d) the trio or a 10% drawdown. For breadth: (a) keep the 50-day 60/40; (b) the 200-day share against StockCharts' 50% line, confirmed by the High-Low Index.
- **Choice:** Trend is Faber's rule on month-end closes, with a daily drawdown badge (Correction 10-20%, Bear market 20%+). Participation is the 200-day share and the High-Low Index both against 50 (Broad, Narrow, Mixed when they disagree), with StockCharts' 30/70 tag on the 50-day share.
- **Why:** each is the published rule, not a fitted one. On 2016-2026 Nifty: Faber changes word 2 times a year against our 16, and marked 89% of the Covid crash and 61% of 2022 as Down with no false Down in 2021. The breadth rule changes 10.5 times a year against 22.6, with no false Narrow in 2021 against 10%. Faber was one of three published trend rules compared and also fits best here; the case for it rests on its testing elsewhere.
- **Cost if wrong:** Faber's word changes only at month end, so a fast fall inside a month shows only in the badge. The evidence is mostly US (1900 on); the only India evidence found covers 1990-2000 (A-063). No rule ensures a fall; each describes conditions under which returns have been lower or more volatile on average.
