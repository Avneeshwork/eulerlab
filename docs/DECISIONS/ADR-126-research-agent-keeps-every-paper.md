# ADR-126: The research agent keeps every paper it reads, says why each was not drafted, and loses fewer to its own checks

- **Context:** The research agent drafted nothing on three days running (research 144): its strict reply check dropped a paper whose section the model wrote as an object, a timeout lost another, off-topic hits used read slots, abstract-less papers were skipped, and nothing recorded why. The operator asked it to find strategies, and to keep adding papers to the library even when a paper is not good.
- **Options:** (a) Keep ADR-084's rule that only approved drafts reach the library. (b) Add every paper the model judged, drafted or not, to `research/papers/`; keep cards behind approval.
- **Choice:** (b). A drafted paper enters with status `idea`, one judged not a trading idea with `rejected` and the reason. Approval links the card to the existing entry instead of refusing. Also: text sections written as objects or lists become lines; one retry of a failed request; candidates read in order of topic-word overlap and skipped with none (A-097); Crossref for a missing abstract (never stored); `run.json` always written; the summary counts read, drafted, not a trading idea, failed and skipped.
- **Why:** The operator asked for (b). A library entry is a record of what was read, not a strategy: it spends no trial, and cards still need `make paper-agent-approve`. It also stops a rejected paper being read again.
- **Cost if wrong:** The library fills with weak papers (17 were `rejected` before; about five a day may be added). The knowledge agent searches them too, and their summaries are model-written from abstracts, marked as not read by the operator. The ranking can skip a relevant paper that shares no word with the topic.
