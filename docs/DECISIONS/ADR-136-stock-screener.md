# ADR-136: A stock screener of long-term (monthly) and swing (weekly) models, each with its backtest and a forward record of its picks

- **Context:** On 2026-11-04 the operator asked for a screener of stocks to buy for the long term and for swing trades, from researched models that are backtested, building on the best existing strategies; long term rebalanced monthly, swing weekly, with a history of past selections and their performance. Every stored strategy is price-based and they are about three bets (HISTORY #44); fundamentals exist as snapshots without publication dates.
- **Options:** (a) Show the existing strategies' picks only. (b) Rank on a hand-made score with no backtest. (c) New models from research 150 on the common engine, fundamentals point in time, plus the best five existing strategies, each list with its stored backtest, gate status and a recorded history.
- **Choice:** (c). `fundamentals/annual.py` builds `fundamentals_annual.jsonl` from each company's newest snapshot (year known 90 days after its end, A-107; joined on ISIN), rebuilt after each weekly fetch. `backtest/screener_models.py`: quality momentum, value-momentum-quality, Piotroski value, a gradient-boosted tree ranker (monthly; Piotroski quarterly) and trend pullback and 52-week breakout (weekly), registered like every strategy and backtested by `make screener-models` (screening runs, no trial). `screener.py` builds today's view as the engine does, runs the six and `config/screener.yaml`'s five existing, writes `screener.json`, appends each model's picks to `screener_history.jsonl` once per day, and scores each past selection from the next close over its holding period (5 or 21 sessions) against NIFTYBEES. Workbench → Screener shows both tabs. `make daily` rebuilds it nightly.
- **Why:** (a) adds nothing new and is one bet. (b) cannot be checked. (c) is checkable twice: by the backtest now and by the recorded picks from today.
- **Cost if wrong:** Fundamental models carry restatement and survivorship bias and a guessed publication lag; the page says so on every list. Lists may be read as advice; each says it is not gate-promoted. The nightly build loads the panel once more (about 1.8 GB, a few minutes).
