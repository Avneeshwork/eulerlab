# ADR-100: The market brief answers first, in one fixed order (the Trader's read)

- **Context:** On 2026-10-31 the operator called the pre-open brief "a joke": it "doesn't make any sense and not helping a trade at all", and asked for "one more component how to answer which give better clarity to trader". The brief listed numbers without saying what they added up to, gave no normal level for any of them (the FII line read like a change but was a share of contracts), showed VIX twice and 22 data grades, left out the option range, max pain, valuation and FII/DII cash flows it already held, and the model's commentary only repeated numbers.
- **Options:** (a) A better model prompt over the same dump. (b) A fixed answer built by rules: bottom line, scorecard with normal levels, today's levels, what would change the read, the books; the model answers three fixed questions under it. (c) A single composite score.
- **Choice:** (b). `agents/trader_read.py` builds it from the inputs the brief already gathers; thresholds are in `config/trader_read.yaml`, editable by the operator. The brief opens with it; the detail follows. Telegram gets the read only. The assistant gets a `market_read` tool and a rule to answer market questions in the same order.
- **Why:** The words must be checkable: each one comes from a number and a threshold on the page, so the operator can disagree with a threshold, not with a model's mood. The model's job shrinks to three questions about the read, and the grounding check (ADR-088) still drops any figure it invents. (c) was rejected for the reason `market_pulse.py` gives: the signals are not added into one score, only counted for the sentence.
- **Cost if wrong:** The thresholds are proposals (A-082). A bad threshold mislabels a row; every row still shows its number and normal range, and nothing reads the words: no strategy, limit or order depends on them.
