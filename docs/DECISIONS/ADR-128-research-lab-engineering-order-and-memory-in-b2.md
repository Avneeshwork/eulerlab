# ADR-128: Research, then the lab, then engineering; the lab tests only research ideas; the agents' memory is kept in B2

- **Context:** The operator, 2026-11-03: "order should be research then lab then engineering", "we have to backtest only what research finds", "all things memory should be retained and store in B2". The lab chose research's topic each day (ADR-111) and tested mostly its own ideas (63 of its last 65 tests); nothing the agents remember was in the bucket; and ADR-127's hand-off never fired (HISTORY #67).
- **Options:** (a) Keep the loop and add a research quota to the lab. (b) A one-way order: research picks its own topic, the lab tests research drafts only, engineering codes what the lab cannot build; a daily backup of the agents' memory to B2.
- **Choice:** (b). Research takes a task's topic, the brief's, or one a day from its own `topics`; the lab's record is passed only as what failed. The lab's `max_ideas` and `model_ideas` are 0. The work runner runs again after the lab, so engineering starts the same evening. `make agents-backup` sends `data/agents/`, the drafts, the papers and the trial ledger, compared by MD5, deleting nothing; `make agents-restore` fills only empty folders.
- **Why:** It is the order the operator set. It also spends far fewer trials (110 in the lab's last three runs, almost all its own ideas), so the bar at the gate rises more slowly. The memory is small (6.9 MB) and was on one disk only.
- **Cost if wrong:** Far fewer backtests a day, perhaps none on a day research drafts nothing buildable; the lab's own idea search is off until the two options are raised. The topic list is chosen by hand and may need changing. Restore depends on B2 keeping earlier versions for an overwritten file (A-098).
