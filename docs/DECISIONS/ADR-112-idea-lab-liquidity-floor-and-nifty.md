# ADR-112: The idea lab's cards have a liquidity floor, and a survivor must beat Nifty

- **Context:** The lab's first run on 2026-11-01 (ADR-111) reported robust Sharpe 5.6 for 21-day low volatility and 3.5 for the share of up days, with no skeptic objection. Its generated cards had no liquidity floor, so a top-30 rank could buy stocks that barely trade, whose flat prices make a curve smooth; `RankAndHold` already records this failure on the real panel (a momentum book buying SPYL on Rs 49,839 a day). Its survival test was only "robust Sharpe above 0 and no critical skeptic finding", which a long-only book passes on the market's own return. The operator agreed to the floor on 2026-11-01 and asked for the lab's work to be audited and fixed.
- **Options:** (a) Leave cards as they are and warn. (b) A turnover floor on generated cards only. (c) A floor on generated cards, the same floor in their IC screen, a floor in what the paper agent drafts, and survival judged against Nifty.
- **Choice:** (c). `config/idea_families.yaml` `min_turnover: 10000000` (Rs 1 crore on the day, A-089) goes into every generated card's configuration and its folder name; `features/ic.evaluate` takes `min_turnover` and `agent_trials` passes the card's lowest floor, so screen and backtest judge the same stocks; the paper agent's prompt asks for the same key. A survivor also needs a positive annualised Sharpe of its daily return over NIFTYBEES on at least 250 shared days (`lab.excess_sharpe`); without Nifty in the panel the old rule stands and the excess is recorded as not measured. Cards tested before the floor stay in the memory file but are left out of planning and lessons (`idea_memory.usable`), so they are planned again with the floor.
- **Why:** A number that comes from untradeable stocks teaches the bandit the wrong family. Beating Nifty is what ADR-108's Romano-Wolf check asks of a candidate anyway, so the lab now says "survived" only for what could pass it.
- **Cost if wrong:** Rs 1 crore is a guess at what makes fills believable (A-089); too high drops small-cap effects the families describe, too low lets thin stocks back in. Re-testing the first run's ideas with the floor spends about 12 more trials. Unfiltered research drafts still run as drafted, because their configurations were fixed before testing.
