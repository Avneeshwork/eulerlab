# ADR-159 · Task cards in plain words; the watchdog stops chasing its own tasks

- **Context:** The operator could not tell from a task card what it was for, why it existed, or what "Accept and close" and "Send back" do; cards showed internal names (`fix_code`, `head_platform`, "split by the model"). Between 5 and 9 Nov the watchdog also raised a new "task overdue" task every day about its own overdue tasks and about engineering fixes only the operator may start, and each was split into fresh near-copies: 13 open tasks about one slow-test problem that engineering's 6 and 8 Nov runs show was already gone.
- **Options:** cards: rename fields in place; or lead each card with plain sentences and move the raw fields behind a toggle. Loop: a per-day dedupe key; skip watchdog-raised tasks and operator-only tasks in the overdue check, and keep one open watchdog task per head and problem.
- **Choice:** each card leads with a plain headline (stacked "Watchdog: task overdue since" prefixes removed), "What it is for" (the skill's own sentence, with chart titles for names), "Why it was raised", "Does it need you? Yes/No" with what to do, and one sentence under every button on what it does (`web/lib/taskWords.ts`, tested). Ids, owner, skill, who planned the steps, arguments, detail and history sit behind "Details for engineers". `watchdog.check_tasks` ignores tasks the watchdog raised and their steps, and assigned tasks whose skill is not `auto`; `ask_for_help` makes no new task while an open watchdog task for the same head and problem exists. The 13 copies were cancelled with a note (they can be retried).
- **Why:** the operator verifies rather than codes, so the card has to answer "do I need to act, and what happens if I press this?" on its own. A watchdog task about a watchdog task, or about work no agent may start, can only add copies.
- **Cost if wrong:** a watchdog task that itself stalls is no longer flagged by the watchdog (it still shows on the head's list and the page), and an operator-only task left unrun is not chased beyond its "Needs you" card.
