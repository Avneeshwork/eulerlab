# ADR-114: The Charts page draws NSE symbols and the macro series from our own data, with our own drawing tools

- **Context:** On 2026-11-01 the seven TradingView macro mini-charts were blank on the operator's Mac and the free Advanced Chart refused `NSE:NIFTYBEES` ("only available on TradingView"). The operator chose "Draw from our own data", then asked for "horizontal vertical lines, interactable, some features of TradingView".
- **Options:** (a) Keep the embeds and accept blanks. (b) Pay for TradingView's licensed Charting Library. (c) Draw from our own bars and macro sweeps with `lightweight-charts` (MIT, already a dependency) and write the tools ourselves.
- **Choice:** (c). NSE symbols show only `OwnChart` (no embed), which reads `/api/history`, the bars a backtest reads. It has a crosshair readout (date, O, H, L, C, change, volume), ranges 1M to All, a log scale, horizontal, vertical and trend lines with erase and clear (kept per symbol in browser storage), and indicators from `web/lib/indicators.ts` (volume, SMA 20/50/200, EMA 20, Bollinger 20/2, RSI 14 and MACD 12/26/9 in panes). The macro charts read `/api/macro/history`, one point per sweep. Other venues keep the TradingView embed.
- **Why:** A chart drawn from the same rows as a result cannot disagree with it, it works with no network, and it costs nothing. The embed is still one click away ("Open on TradingView").
- **Cost if wrong:** Drawings live in one browser and are lost when its storage is cleared. The macro charts are only as long as our sweep history (they start on the first day `macro` was kept, HISTORY #52) and show gaps where the sweep did not run. The indicators are ours; a difference from TradingView's figures is ours to explain (`web/lib/indicators.test.ts` pins the formulas).
