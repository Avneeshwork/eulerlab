# ADR-188 — Unusual old-year prices: checked against the bhavcopy, kept unless proved wrong

**Date:** 2026-11-14. **Status:** accepted for the copy in `data/derived/rebuild-2026-11-14/`; the live panel has no mask. A-146, research 195.

- **Context.** The 2000-2014 rows hold about 3,270 one-day moves over 40%. Some are splits NSE wrote in older wording the parser missed. The operator's rule (2026-11-14): check every unusual price, keep it as NSE published it unless a check proves it wrong, and leave universe filters such as a minimum price to each strategy.
- **Options.** (a) Exclude windows around unexplained jumps and drop prints that revert. (b) Check each unusual row against the bhavcopy itself and exclude only a row that fails.
- **Choice.** (b), `features/panel_mask.py`. The parser reads "Fv Split Rs.10/- To Rs.2/-" and its variants; splits and bonuses of companies without an ISIN are keyed to their own id through the no-ISIN master; a pre-2015 price factor is kept only when the move across its ex-date matches within 10%. A move a confirmed factor does not explain is checked: open and close inside [low, high]; turnover over volume inside [low, high]; the next session's PREVCLOSE equal to this close unless a corporate action falls between. A failing row is excluded with its reason, known when its evidence existed; every other row stays and is flagged "unusual, unverified". Moves are measured from the last kept close. `MarketView.__post_init__` applies `data/derived/panel_mask.jsonl` when it exists.
- **Why.** The panel stays what the exchange printed, every exclusion names the check that failed, and a decision before the evidence existed still sees the printed row.
- **Cost if wrong.** A move with no PREVCLOSE to check (the last row before a delisting) can only be flagged. The tolerances are hand-chosen (A-146). Readers that bypass `MarketView` (the vector screens) do not apply the mask yet.
