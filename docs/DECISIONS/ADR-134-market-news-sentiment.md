# ADR-134: The brief reads market-wide and geopolitical news, scores its tone locally, has its impact judged, and shows crash risk from history

- **Context:** On 2026-11-04 the operator said crashes are hard to predict and the brief takes no news, so it misses geopolitical and country-wide events; they asked for news sentiment and possible impact at the end of the brief, using their Currents API key and an open-source FinBERT. The brief held only per-instrument Google News headlines, unscored.
- **Options:** (a) A paid sentiment feed. (b) FinBERT alone on every headline. (c) Currents for headlines and summaries; a FinBERT-style model run locally for tone; one model request to judge impact for India on a few stories per theme; crash odds from history.
- **Choice:** (c). `ingest/market_news.py` runs ten fixed searches (`config/market_news.yaml`), lands under `news/market/` and keeps `data/derived/market_news.jsonl` with relevance and tone; `forecast/news_tone.py` scores with `mrm8488/distilroberta-finetuned-financial-news-sentiment-analysis` pinned at `ae0eab9` (Apache-2.0, `uv sync --extra news`), picks up to 12 stories (no market recaps, geopolitics and policy first), and checks each judged line (story number, fixed words, figures only from the story). The brief ends with "News and possible impact" (tone against the last 30 days, by theme, a SHOCK RISK line for high-severity bad news, each judged story with sectors and why, and where tone and judgement disagree) and "Crash risk (history, not a forecast)": how often Nifty fell 2% and 3% at today's VIX band, and how often the close broke below the band. Telegram gets one news line. Market brief requests rise from 6 to 12 a day.
- **Why:** (a) costs money and the free tiers miss India or history (research 148). (b) misreads economics: "crude falls" scores negative (research 148). (c) uses what the operator already has, keeps every score repeatable, and keeps the model's judgement checked and labelled.
- **Cost if wrong:** The impact judgement can be wrong in substance; it is labelled as a judgement and changes no number. About 1.2 GB of disk for PyTorch and the model; about 40 Currents requests a day of 250; one more Groq request per brief. News does not enter the forecast until a test shows it helps (T-4433).
