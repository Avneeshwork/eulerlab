# ADR-188 — Weak signals are combined into one walk-forward forecast and traded only where it beats cost

**Date:** 2026-11-14. **Status:** accepted; operator's "do this also" on 2026-11-14 for the list in issue #161's follow-up (#155 first). Tested on made-up data only.

- **Context.** Every rule here had to pay a 12 bps intraday round trip alone, and 124 price rules never did. A signal with an IC of 0.02 was thrown away, though the fundamental law (IR ≈ IC × √breadth) says many such signals together can pay. Issue #155; Kakushadze, "How to Combine a Billion Alphas" (arXiv 1603.05937).
- **Options.** (a) Keep judging one rule at a time. (b) A combiner over language-v2 formulas: walk-forward monthly weights (mean IC or ridge, shrunk to equal), a no-trade band equal to the round trip's own cost, and the rule engine's books and checks for the result. (c) A machine-learned model (gradient boosting) over all fields now.
- **Choice.** (b). `research/combine.py`: each signal is ranked and z-scored per session; the target is open of t to close of t+h-1 net of the session's mean and of its sector's mean when sectors are known; each month's weights use only sessions whose target closed before the month began (embargo of h); weights are shrunk halfway to equal size (A-144), then scaled to returns by a one-number fit on the same past. A stock is entered only while |forecast| exceeds that day's round-trip cost on ₹1 lakh (12.2 bps intraday, 26 bps delivery); swing long only. The book is a `Combined` rule with a fingerprint, screened through `rule_engine.Engine`'s books (halves, 2x costs, bias checks) and recorded as one experiment-store row with daily returns. The research loop gets `combine_signals`; the operator gets `make combine`.
- **Why.** It turns signals that failed alone into one testable book, with every fit out of sample and the cost bar applied per trade rather than per rule. Per-session sums make a month's fit an addition, so a 20-signal book over ten years fits in seconds.
- **Cost if wrong.** A combined book is not a card yet: it cannot reach the gate or a paper book, and the forward shadow cannot replay it (S-072). Residuals are to the cross-section and sector, not to a Nifty beta. Each combine is one trial in the firewall, but the model may try several combinations a night, each a trial. No real-data note yet (T-4607).
