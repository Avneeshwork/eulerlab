# ADR-106: The brief shows what history says, from three fixed conditions

- **Context:** Research 134 found good notes take the outside view: what usually followed similar setups, with the number of cases. The operator accepted it as PR 2 of the brief rebuild on 2026-10-31, along with each book's backtest in this kind of market.
- **Options:** (a) A similarity search over many features to find the "closest" past days. (b) A few fixed conditions, exact match, counts shown. (c) A model's judgement of similar periods.
- **Choice:** (b). Three conditions, written in `config/history_setup.yaml` before any result was looked at: Nifty's side of its 200-day average; India VIX low, middle or high against its previous 251 closes; who leads over 21 sessions. For 1, 5 and 20 sessions ahead the brief shows Nifty's median, share up and worst 10%, with matching days and episodes. Under 30 days it shows no numbers. Each running book gets its own stored backtest's return on the session after each matching day. Code: `agents/history_says.py`. Pages: `docs/METRICS/market/history-base-rates.md`, `performance/books-in-this-market.md`.
- **Why:** (a) and (c) would let the matching be tuned until the history looks good, a search with no trial count. Exact matches on three named conditions can be checked by hand, and their sample size is plain. Conditions use only closes up to the day; a test proves it.
- **Cost if wrong:** The conditions may be the wrong ones (A-087). If they are changed, it must be a decision, not a tune, and the old ones are recorded. The episodes count shows when many matching days are really one stretch. It logs no trial and touches neither `gates.py` nor `mandate.yaml`, because it evaluates no strategy configuration.
