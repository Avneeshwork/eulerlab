# Company and GitHub strategy screen for NSE intraday research

**Screen date:** 2026-10-15
**Scope:** Public company disclosures, an investor pitchbook, and public GitHub strategy repositories. Focus is Indian listed equities and intraday rules. This is a source screen, not a profitability claim or a backtest.

## What the sources actually establish

Public prop-firm materials reveal strategy families and operating constraints more often than executable signals. Optiver describes continuous two-sided market making, multi-asset hedging, ETF creation/redemption, and opening/closing/VWAP execution benchmarks. These are descriptions of the firm's business and mechanisms; they do not disclose a retail-tradable alpha rule or prove that an NSE adaptation is profitable. [Optiver, what we do](https://www.optiver.com/what-we-do/), [ETF creation/redemption](https://prod-www.optiver.com/insights/explainers/etf-creationredemption-and-authorised-participants/), [IMC 2024 annual report](https://www.imc.com/in/corporate-news/IMC-launches-2024-annual-report).

The QSIF investor deck describes high-frequency analytics, real-time liquidity/regime indicators, statistical arbitrage, and volatility arbitrage as capabilities or past experience. The Samssara pitchbook gives a more concrete NSE stock-futures workflow: screen liquid names, group by sub-sector, form pairs and baskets, test mean reversion, monitor neutrality, and unwind intraday. Those are useful research leads, but the pitchbook is a self-reported historical presentation hosted on Scribd; its performance claims are not independently verified. [QSIF deck](https://www.qsif.com/Admin/Pdf/qsif_Deck.pdf), [Samssara pitchbook](https://www.scribd.com/presentation/53601391/Samssara-Capital-Stat-Arb-Investors-Pitchbook).

Several share broad families with existing work; I mark those overlaps below rather than treating a new parameterization as a new strategy. GitHub code and README results are leads only: repository authors' results are not independently verified here.

## Local backtest results

Eight candidates with sufficient NSE candle data were coded in `src/eulerlab/intraday/company_github_screen.py` and evaluated at fixed settings. The shared training period was 492 sessions in 2022–2023; the evaluation period was 224 common sessions from 2024-01-01 through 2024-11-30. Positions use the next traded minute open, ₹2.5 lakh notional per name, date-aware NSE intraday charges, 2 bp assumed slippage per side, and a 15:10 square-off. Each candidate is capped at one trade per stock/session except the selector, which permits one per predeclared segment. No later data was read.

| Candidate | Gross return / Sharpe | Net return / Sharpe | Trades | Estimated charges + slippage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bollinger squeeze expansion | +0.59% / 0.375 | **-18.46% / -12.622** | 895 | ₹209,874 |
| Initial-balance failure fade | -0.68% / -0.341 | **-17.17% / -9.568** | 774 | ₹181,498 |
| Training-selected time-of-day switch | +3.51% / 2.009 | **-42.78% / -31.683** | 2,525 | ₹592,136 |
| Value-area acceptance breakout (HLC3 proxy) | -1.07% / -0.673 | **-18.94% / -13.191** | 849 | ₹199,094 |
| Edge-to-edge traversal (HLC3 proxy) | -0.78% / -0.445 | **-12.03% / -7.153** | 513 | ₹120,286 |
| Low-volume new-low exhaustion | +0.74% / 0.491 | **-18.13% / -13.060** | 884 | ₹207,300 |
| 80% value-area rule (HLC3 proxy) | +4.26% / 2.329 | **-1.44% / -0.811** | 240 | ₹56,289 |
| POC reclaim/rotation (HLC3 proxy) | +1.24% / 0.974 | **-7.67% / -6.071** | 393 | ₹92,169 |

All eight lost after estimated costs in this four-stock screen. The 80% rule had the least-negative net result, but its source study reports that the advertised traversal rate fails, and this local run uses approximate profile levels. Treat it as a negative-control result, not as a profitable strategy. The adaptive switch's positive gross result was overwhelmed by turnover costs. These are narrow screens on four names, not evidence that all NSE stocks behave the same way.

The remaining two candidates were not backtested because the local history has no timestamped NSE/BSE quote pairs, full order-book updates, order queue positions, or partial-fill records. To test the cross-venue rule, acquire synchronized NSE and BSE quote/trade history with sequence/timestamp quality and venue cost schedules. To test passive market making, acquire depth deltas and signed trade prints plus latency/queue-aware replay or verified execution logs. Minute OHLCV cannot substitute for either feed.

## Company and repository source records

The company materials are strategy-family evidence only. None publishes enough details to recreate a proprietary Indian-stock intraday alpha rule. The strongest direct company documentation found is the QSIF pitch deck for its stated capabilities, Optiver's material for market-making and ETF mechanisms, and Share India's annual report for its proprietary HFT desk and retail algo platform. [Share India annual report 2024–25](https://www.shareindia.com/wp-content/uploads/data/uploads/Investor_Relations_Files/annual_report_2024-25.pdf).

Eight hypotheses have fixed, cost-aware screens in the local result file; all eight are net negative in the reported evaluation window. The value-area, edge-to-edge, 80% and POC rules are OHLCV proxy calculations, not true volume-at-price results. The two quote-driven strategies remain blocked pending the feeds listed above. There is no supported profitability claim from this screen.
