# Ten Indian equity intraday candidates outside the current strategy cards

**Screen date:** 2026-10-15
**Scope:** Indian listed stocks, intraday signals, excluding strategies already represented in `src/eulerlab/strategies/studies.py` and the previously tested families in research notes 076–085.

Source pages were checked on 2026-10-15. The local results are generated at `data/derived/stock_intraday_candidates.json`.
Reproduce the three price-only screens with `make stock-intraday-candidates`, then import their curves with `make library-sync`.

This is a shortlist of research hypotheses, not ten proven profitable systems. Several sources establish predictability or market behavior rather than a net-profitable trading rule. I have labeled those as adaptations and called out the data they require. The source papers report results from different periods and markets; none establishes expected profitability on current NSE stocks.

## Candidates

| # | Candidate and fixed hypothesis to test | Research basis | Duplication screen and local feasibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Single-stock futures lead cash:** when a stock future's one-minute return leads its cash share by a stable, out-of-sample lag, trade the cash share in that direction on the next eligible bar; skip if the lead is not significant in the training window. | Indian study of 30 liquid stocks tests one-minute futures and underlying returns, 2017–2019. [Goa University repository record](https://irgu.unigoa.ac.in/drs/handle/unigoa/6287). | No futures-to-cash strategy card. Cash minute bars exist; historical single-stock futures minute bars are not in the current archive. **Data blocked.** |
| 2 | **Liquid-stock leader to laggard:** within predeclared bank and IT pairs, trade the peer when the more liquid member makes a five-minute move that the peer has not yet matched; exit at a fixed short horizon. | A 2007 study measures daily co-movement among 201 NSE stocks and finds market-wide movement dominates sector clusters. A separate 2018 study uses 30-second NSE price data to identify bank, IT, auto, and energy clusters, but does not publish an executable trading rule. [Daily cross-correlation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.2115), [high-frequency clustering study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09514). | Different from same-stock momentum and the library's cross-sectional extreme fade. The high-frequency source suggests groups, not a profitable leader-laggard rule. The local screen uses fixed bank and IT pairs; **data-feasible as an adaptation**, with weak breadth. |
| 3 | **Intraday pair-spread convergence:** form pairs using only a rolling training window; enter when the standardized log-price spread crosses a fixed threshold, exit at its mean or a fixed time stop, and trade both legs together. | High-frequency equity-pairs research on FTSE 100 reports that returns are highly sensitive to costs and execution delay, with most returns in the first hour. [Bowen, Hutchinson & O'Sullivan (2010)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1611623). | The existing India pair paper is daily, and no intraday pair card exists. Minute bars permit an India adaptation. The source's one-period execution delay erased returns, so next-bar fills and full two-leg NSE costs are essential. **Data-feasible; high cost risk.** |
| 4 | **Lead-lag-aware pairs:** select pairs from a training window using a lead-lag distance, then trade the lagging leg after a leader move only when the pair relationship remains stable; close when the lag catches up or the spread invalidates. | A pair-selection paper explicitly incorporates lead-lag relationships when choosing stocks for pairs trading. [Lead-lag pair selection study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.05057). | Distinct from #3's synchronous spread-z-score entry: this uses delayed response as the signal and needs a rolling pair-selection process. No matching strategy card. Existing minute data can test it, but selection must be refit point-in-time. **Data-feasible as an adaptation.** |
| 5 | **Deep-book pressure:** use imbalance across multiple NSE order-book levels (not only best bid/ask) to forecast the next short-horizon mid-price move; trade only when predicted movement exceeds spread and costs. | Five years of NSE order-book and trade-book data (2014–2018) found deeper levels contributed to short-term return predictability after controls. [Indian open limit-order-book study](https://doaj.org/article/f889de21e919474daf8c846021452ece). | Not a candle indicator or existing card. Current OHLCV lacks book snapshots, and the study's predictability is not proof of net returns. **Data blocked.** |
| 6 | **Signed order-flow continuation:** estimate buy-initiated minus sell-initiated traded value over a short window; trade in its direction only during high-liquidity periods and after checking the effect beyond bid–ask bounce. | Indian NSE research finds order imbalance can explain short-term returns after controlling for bid–ask bounce, with stronger predictability in high-liquidity regimes. [Information content of order imbalance](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612320316779). | Separate from OHLCV volume-surge breakouts: it requires trade signing. No matching card. Local bars do not identify aggressor side. **Data blocked.** |
| 7 | **Order-size versus order-count divergence:** distinguish large directional order size from a broad increase in small order count; follow the former and fade the latter over a short horizon. | Intraday transaction data for Indian cross-listed firms found positive price response to larger order size but negative response to a greater number of orders in NSE; the authors associate these with information-based versus countervailing flow. [Indian cross-listed order-flow study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1042443111000229). | A distinct microstructure hypothesis, not a price-only reversal. Needs signed order-level data and fees/spread. No matching card. **Data blocked.** |
| 8 | **Pre-open auction volatility gate:** measure pre-open auction information/imbalance, then allow an existing intraday signal only on sessions whose auction state forecasts elevated first-15-minute realized volatility; the auction signal chooses whether to trade, not direction. | Indian NSE pre-open research reports that auction information affects realized volatility after the open, but does not report return direction. [Bag (2019), call-auction information](https://apcz.umk.pl/CJFA/article/view/30366). | No strategy card uses auction state. Keep this as a risk/trade-selection gate, not a directional edge. Historical pre-open order snapshots are unavailable here. **Data blocked.** |
| 9 | **Scheduled earnings reaction-speed trade:** for timestamped earnings releases during continuous trading, test a fixed first-minute direction-following entry with a strict 5–10 minute time exit; separately report overnight releases and exclude the opening auction. | Patell & Wolfson find the initial response to earnings/dividend announcements appears within a few price changes and simple-rule returns dissipate within 5–10 minutes. [Stanford working paper](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/working-papers/intraday-speed-adjustment-stock-prices-earnings-dividend). | Event-time entry is absent from the existing cards. This source gives a strong negative prior for delayed entry, not a positive profit claim. Needs reliable historical release timestamps and announcement classifications. **Data blocked pending event data.** |
| 10 | **News-attention diffusion trade gate:** only activate a short-horizon news response when attention to a stock's article is arriving gradually across market participants; test direction separately from the attention signal and exit within a fixed short horizon. | Fedyk's study finds dispersion in when finance professionals read news predicts volume surges within minutes of individual articles. It uses proprietary news-click data and does not itself establish directional returns. [AEA paper abstract](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2019/preliminary/940). | No news/attention card exists. This is a research adaptation, not a ready-to-trade signal; article timestamps, text, and a defensible attention proxy are required. **Data blocked.** |

## Price-only screens

Candidates **2, 3, and 4** were screened on the local NSE one-minute archive. The fixed universe is HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK, TCS, and INFY. Training used 492 common sessions from 2022–2023; evaluation used 224 common sessions from 2024-01-01 through 2024-11-30. No later data was opened. Signals use completed bars and enter at the next traded minute open; positions are closed by 15:10. Costs use the date-aware NSE intraday fee router plus assumed slippage of 2 basis points per side. Daily returns include zero-return sessions and are measured on fixed ₹10 lakh capital.

| Candidate | Gross return / Sharpe | Net return / Sharpe | Max drawdown, net | Trades / fills | Estimated costs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Liquid-stock leader to laggard | -0.12% / -0.081 | **-14.03% / -10.401** | -14.03% | 355 / 710 | ₹149,896 |
| Intraday pair-spread convergence | +9.21% / +2.778 | **-40.84% / -18.682** | -40.84% | 1,306 / 5,224 | ₹612,513 |
| Training-selected lead-lag pair | +0.82% / +0.294 | **-12.54% / -4.494** | -13.28% | 178 / 356 | ₹142,130 |

The pair-spread rule's small gross gain did not cover repeated two-leg costs. The training-selected pair was TCS leading INFY at a 10-minute lag, but its training correlation was only 0.0098. These screens reject all three rules as profitable under the stated fills and costs; I did not tune them after seeing the result. They remain small fixed-universe studies, not evidence about the full NSE universe.

Candidates **1 and 5–10** need additional futures, order-book, auction, event, or news data before a faithful test. Candidate 6 remains blocked until signed trades are available; OHLCV volume cannot substitute for order imbalance.
