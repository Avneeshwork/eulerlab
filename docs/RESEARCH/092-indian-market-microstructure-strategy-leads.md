# Further Indian intraday strategy leads from market-microstructure research

**Screen date:** 2026-10-15
**Scope:** New research leads that are not already represented as implemented strategies. This is a source and data-feasibility screen; no new local backtest was run.

## Shortlist

| # | Hypothesis | Source evidence | Deduplication and test status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Repeated institutional-flow continuation:** estimate a stock's net institutional order-submission direction in each half-hour, then trade the next day's same half-hour in that direction; close at that interval's end. | Murphy and Thirumalai report same-half-hour return predictability on subsequent days from repetitive institutions' half-hour net order activity in NSE data. | **New rule, data blocked.** Needs masked trader IDs, trader class, and order submissions. OHLCV cannot reconstruct who initiated or repeated the flow. Highest-priority source-faithful lead if exchange-grade data is available. [Paper](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jfir.12131) |
| 2 | **Algorithmic-trader timing replication:** identify the timing component of algorithm-tagged order flow at 1- and 5-minute horizons, then test whether a public observable proxy retains the return after cost. | A study of 169 NSE stocks reports positive intraday market-timing performance for algorithmic traders at one- and five-minute intervals. | **New research question, data blocked.** The paper's edge is measured using trader-type data and VWAP decomposition; it does not disclose a public OHLCV rule. Need tagged order/trade data and careful separation of timing from execution skill. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ribaf.2020.101283) |
| 3 | **Auction-phase pressure / price-discovery model:** model whether indicative equilibrium-price movement and imbalance during CAS predict final auction-price error versus its 15-minute VWAP reference. | NSE's CAS sets a separate auction for eligible stocks and disseminates indicative equilibrium price, executable quantity, and imbalance; its historical-report page lists CAS archives. | **New measurement candidate, not yet an intraday return strategy.** CAS began 3 August 2026 and has a short sample. A useful model may improve execution or explain price formation; a signal to trade the auction close and unwind next open crosses overnight and does not qualify as intraday. Requires timestamped IEP/imbalance history, not merely final daily rows. [NSE CAS rules](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/closing-auction-session), [NSE CAS archive index](https://betanseapi.nseindia.com/resources/historical-reports-capital-market-daily-monthly-archives) |
| 4 | **Day-trader-flow contrarian strategy:** use account-tagged aggressive flow from consistently unprofitable day traders as a same-session liquidity-provision/contrarian signal. | The NBER study of BSE trade data reports day traders lose on average to counterparties and incur a substantial immediacy cost. | **New but data blocked and high risk of selection bias.** Requires BSE trader identities, reliable trader profitability classification, and a point-in-time rule. The average loss of a participant group does not imply a tradable signal. [NBER paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31127) |

## Additional leads screened and deduplicated

| Lead | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| NSE open-limit-book imbalance by trader type | Proprietary algo limit-order imbalance carries price-discovery information in NSE stocks. | Related to the existing order-imbalance research in 087 and requires trader-tagged depth snapshots; no independent OHLCV candidate. [Study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0927539819300532) |
| Hourly order imbalance | A 2026 NSE study finds persistent hourly imbalances and gross return prediction, but says the simple strategy loses its abnormal return after costs. | Source-level negative control; the existing order-flow candidate already records this data family. Do not count gross-only results. [Study](https://www.ubplj.org/index.php/jpm/article/view/2235) |
| Intraday volume and absolute-return relationship | NSE tick data shows a positive contemporaneous relationship between volume and price movement, with a stronger positive-return/volume association. | Not predictive direction on its own; it overlaps volume-surge and momentum rules already screened. Treat as a possible risk/participation feature only after incremental testing. [Study](https://doi.org/10.1177/0972652720930586) |
| Bid-ask-spread and volume information | A 50-stock NSE five-minute study finds contemporaneous relationships among absolute returns, volume and spread. | Spread is absent from our OHLCV archive and the abstract does not establish a costed directional rule. Data blocked; not a new standalone strategy. [Study](https://ideas.repec.org/a/asi/aeafrj/v6y2016i3p135-150id1470.html) |
| Opening price discovery after call-auction adoption | The source studies how the pre-open auction changes opening-price discovery, with effects varying by liquidity. | Already represented by the call-auction source and opening/gap strategy families in research 087 and paper `bag-2019-call-auction-information-india`; no duplicate card. [Study](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/10.1080/23322039.2014.944668) |
| U-shaped volume, volatility and trade counts | NSE observations show more activity at the open and close; controlling for volume changes the apparent intraday volatility curve. | Descriptive seasonality, not a directional edge. Time-of-day strategies and opening/closing effects are already in the library. [Study](https://doi.org/10.1177/0972652720930586) |
| Price and trade-size clustering | NSE trades cluster at particular prices and share sizes, including multiples of 500 shares. | Could inform execution or limit-price placement, but does not specify return direction or a complete strategy. Requires individual trades/order book and is not counted. [Study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.qref.2017.11.006) |
| CAS pressure to next opening | Compare the final CAS price/imbalance with the next session's opening print. | This is an overnight event strategy, not intraday; CAS history is also too short for a stable profitability claim. Keep outside the five-strategy target. [SEBI circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jan-2026/introduction-of-closing-auction-session-cas-in-the-equity-cash-segment-and-certain-modifications-in-the-pre-open-auction-session_99122.html) |

## Decision

The first two hypotheses are the best new sources for a later data-acquisition decision, but neither can be honestly backtested with the current five-stock OHLCV archive. The CAS work can begin as a market-data study when a useful sequence of timestamped auction snapshots exists; its post-auction return must be kept separate because it spans the night. The other leads either duplicate existing work, fail after stated costs, lack a specified directional edge, or need unavailable microstructure fields.

No candidate in this note counts toward the five profitable-strategy goal. Do not create synthetic signed volume from OHLCV or treat an aggregate market finding as a profitable per-stock rule. Any future test needs a fixed rule, point-in-time eligible universe, next-observable-price execution, NSE fees, spread/slippage estimates, and an untouched evaluation window.
